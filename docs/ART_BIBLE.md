# Art Bible — КАЧАЛКА

## Direction

Night neighbourhood gym. Chunky low-poly, **iconic machines**, hero readable at 8 m. Atmosphere from colour and neon, not from polygon density.

References (language, not assets): Lethal Company readability, Mega Man X chunky props, Russian 24h basement gym (качалка) — rubber floor, chrome barbell, cheap neon.

## Shape language

- Machines: rectangles + cylinders. No filigree.
- Hero: trapezoid torso, cylinder limbs, sphere head. Joints must read.
- Growth: width, not height. Head locked.

## Lighting (engine)

- Directional moon-white, energy 0.55, 35°
- Omni cyan above pull-up, omni magenta above dumbbells
- Ambient 0.08 charcoal
- No GI on web

## Camera language

Gameplay: 35 mm equivalent, FOV 55°, over-right-shoulder. Screenshot/marketing: ¾ front-right, elevation 18°.

## Silhouette test

Flat grey material, camera at 8 m. Must identify: bench, squat cage, dumbbell rack, treadmill, pull-up, hero. If not — simplify, do not add detail.
