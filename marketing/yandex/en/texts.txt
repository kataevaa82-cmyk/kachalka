description
Just one more rep. Then one more. Can you finish a set without a single miss?

Start as a 62 kg rookie and work your way to the Titan Forge. Explore a 3D gym in first person, pick a station and time each move for a perfect rep. Precision earns muscle mass and in-game cash, while combos make every clean hit count.

• 15 stations: bench press, dumbbells, squats, pull-ups, cardio and more.
• Different mini-games: time your presses, alternate hands, remember sequences and keep your balance.
• 6 progression tiers with faster rhythms, tighter timing windows and bigger rewards.
• Quests, protein and equipment bought with in-game currency.
• Rest, showers and a sauna between demanding sets.
• Mass leaderboard and an original soundtrack.

Three misses end your set. One perfect rep can start your next streak. How far will your combo go?

how_to_play
Walk up to a station and start a set. Follow its prompt: hit the green zone on the bench press, alternate hands with dumbbells, or master the sequence and balance challenges at other stations. Perfect reps earn more mass and in-game cash. Three misses end the set.

Desktop: WASD to walk, mouse to look, E to interact, Space to perform a rep. Some exercises use A/D or Q/W/E/R; follow the on-screen hints. Esc pauses between sets.

Mobile: use the left stick to walk, swipe on the right to look, and tap the on-screen action buttons. Hold your device in landscape.

Watch your energy and fatigue. Recover on a bench, in the shower or sauna, and buy upgrades at the bar. Gain mass to unlock more stations and higher gym tiers.

In the pause menu: leaderboard, credits, and sound and music toggles.
